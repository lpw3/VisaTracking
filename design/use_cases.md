## Use Cases

1. The Visa-Seeker 
- Calculate an estimated wait time for their specific visa based on historical data
- See changes in wait times to see stability of the estimate.
- See statistics on chances of approval.
- See any updates to visa criteria.
2. The Immigration Expert
- View aggregate information across countries on a specific visa.
- Compare incoming population across countries and visa types.
